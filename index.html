How it works

  1. Extract. Claude reads the teaser or CIM (PDFs supported) and returns a fixed schema: historical and projected financials, customer concentration, recurring revenue, the seller's ask, plus a thesis, risks with mitigants, and diligence questions. It's told not to compute returns or recommend.
  2. Screen in code. EBITDA size, margin floor, revenue growth, top-customer concentration and recurring revenue, each checked against set thresholds.
  3. Model in code. A 5-year LBO on LTM EBITDA (historicals only; management projections are shown but never drive returns), with a cash sweep, MOIC, IRR, and a 5×5 entry/exit sensitivity table.
  4. Price it. Solves for the highest entry multiple that still clears the target IRR, which gives an indicative bid range to compare against the seller's ask.
